Government of Hungary
Full Scholarship | Tuition + Stipend + Housing | Hungary
Deadline: January 15, 2026
Description

The most prestigious higher education scholarship program of the Hungarian Government. It aims to support the internationalization of Hungarian higher education.

What is covered:
  • Tuition-free education.
  • Monthly Stipend:
    • BA/MA: 43,700 HUF (approx. €110)
    • PhD: 140,000 - 180,000 HUF (approx. €350-450)
  • Housing: Free dormitory place OR 40,000 HUF/month contribution.
  • Medical Insurance (Social security + private).
Eligible Countries

The program is based on bilateral cooperation agreements. Currently, almost 90 partners across 5 continents are involved.

Africa (e.g., Nigeria, Egypt...) Asia (e.g., China, India, Pakistan...) Europe (e.g., Ukraine, Serbia...) Americas (e.g., Brazil, Mexico...)
See the Full Official List of Partners
Requirements
  • Double Nomination: Must apply online and be nominated by the sending partner authority in your home country.
  • Language: Proof of language proficiency (usually English B2 or C1).
  • Documents: School certificates, medical certificate, copy of passport.
