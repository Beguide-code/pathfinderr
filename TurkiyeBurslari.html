Türkiye Bursları
Republic of Türkiye
Full Scholarship | Tuition + Stipend + Flight | Türkiye
Deadline: February 20, 2026
Description

Türkiye Burslari is a government-funded, competitive scholarship program, awarded to outstanding students and researchers to pursue full-time or short-term programs at the top universities in Türkiye.

What is covered:
  • Tuition-free university placement.
  • Monthly Stipend: Paid monthly for BA, MA, and PhD levels (amounts adjusted annually).
  • Accommodation: Free state dormitory placement.
  • Flight Ticket: One-off round-trip flight ticket (Start & End of studies).
  • Language: 1-year free Turkish Language Course (TÖMER).
  • Health Insurance: Full coverage.
Eligible Countries

The program is open to citizens of all countries worldwide.

Requirements
  • Academic Merit: Min. 70% (Undergraduate), 75% (Graduate), 90% (Health Sciences).
  • Age Limit: Under 21 (BA), Under 30 (MA), Under 35 (PhD).
  • Documents: Diploma/Transcripts, ID/Passport, Letter of Intent.
